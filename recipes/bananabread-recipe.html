BANANA BREAD RECIPE

SIMPLE BANANA BREAD

I developed this simple banana bread while living in a 10-foot trailer with limited ingredients. Made without added oil, butter, or baking soda, it bakes up dense and bread-like rather than soft and cake-like, with plenty of banana flavor.

Ingredients

  • cooking spray
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 4 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2/3 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease an 8x4-inch loaf pan and set aside.
  2. Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl. Set aside.
  3. Mash bananas in another bowl and stir in eggs and sugar.
  4. Stir the flour mixture into the banana mixture just to combine, then fold in walnuts. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of the bread comes out clean, 55 to 65 minutes.
  6. Remove from the oven and cool on a rack for 10 minutes, then remove from the pan and cool completely. Slice and serve.
